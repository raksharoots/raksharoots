# **App Name**: RakshaRoots Landing

## Core Features:

- Product Display: Display details and prices for 'Raksha Roots Face Mask' and 'Raksha Roots Body Scrub'.
- Future Products Teaser: Highlight the upcoming product line with a visually appealing 'Coming Soon' section.
- Contact Information: Show instagram @raksharoots and email - raksharoots@gmail.com contact information.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Light pink (#F2D7D5) from the RakshaRoots logo.
- Secondary color: Soft red (#E0B8B1), complementing the primary.
- Accent color: Warm yellow (#F4E2D8) to highlight key information and CTAs.
- Body and headline font: 'Alegreya', serif, offers an elegant and intellectual touch.
- Ensure a clean and intuitive product showcase, creating an immersive browsing experience.
- Incorporate fluid, scroll-triggered animations to reveal content and enrich the user experience. Examples of such animations include parallax scrolling of images.
- Apply subtle animations on hover interactions for product elements and CTAs, offering instant visual feedback and enhanced interactivity.
